I was afraid to open the letter. My hands trembled as I stood alone in the empty station. I felt nervous and terrified about what I might find inside.

I remembered the last evening at home. I felt so sad and lonely after our argument. I missed my sister and the familiar sound of her laughter.

Then I remembered her final words. I was furious that she had blamed me for everything. I felt angry and betrayed, and I wanted to shout that it was unfair.

I finally opened the envelope. I was astonished to find a photograph instead of an angry message. I felt completely surprised by the unexpected picture of us as children.

On the back she had written that she loved me. I felt such love and affection for her that I held the photograph close to my heart. She was still my family, and I cared for her deeply.

I felt happy and joyful as I reached for my phone. I was delighted that we could start again. For the first time in weeks, I smiled and felt grateful for a new beginning.
